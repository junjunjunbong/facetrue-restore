## Brand cover prompt

```text
Use case: ads-marketing. Asset: a premium, wide landscape GitHub README cover for FaceTrue Restore, a local reference-guided video restoration prototype. Design an understated, elegant product brand image inspired by an archival film room, not a generic AI ad. Palette: near-black charcoal #131513 and dark olive #1b1e1b, warm ivory #edece1, muted gold #d7bd83, muted sage #a7ada1. Composition: calm generous margins, balanced negative space, polished editorial hierarchy. On the left, render the exact, highly legible product name 'FaceTrue Restore' in large warm ivory refined modern typography, and below it the exact smaller line 'See what one photo changes.' On the right, create a sophisticated dimensional still life of a short strip of archival film, a single small framed reference-photo card represented by an abstract faceless silhouette, and a fine golden vertical comparison line. The physical materials should feel tactile: glass, matte paper, old film grain, very restrained gold highlights. The mood is respectful, intimate, quiet, precise, and confident. This is conceptual artwork, so do not depict any real person, do not show realistic restored faces, and do not create a before-and-after claim. No interface screenshot, no graphs, no metrics, no extra text, no watermarks, no platform logos, no bright neon. Professional art direction and excellent typographic spacing, horizontal layout suitable for a README hero.
```
